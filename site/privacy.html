Pigeonslip

Privacy

The short version: nothing about you or your labels leaves your computer.

The app

Pigeonslip runs entirely on your PC. It has no network access, no accounts, no analytics and no crash reporting. It reads only the files you drop in or pick, and writes only the PDFs you choose to save. It remembers three settings (paper size, trimming, cut lines) on your computer.

This website

This site is static pages served by Cloudflare. It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, ads or third-party scripts. Cloudflare keeps standard server logs to run and protect the service. The download button takes you to GitHub, which has its own privacy policy.
