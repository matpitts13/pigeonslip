Print shipping labels at 4 × 6, two to a page.

Label downloads from TikTok Shop, eBay and Etsy often put one label on a whole letter page, too big to tape on a box. Drop them into Pigeonslip. It trims each one to the standard USPS 4 × 6 in size and fits as many as it can on each sheet.

Download for Windows Source code

Free and open source (MIT). Windows 10 and 11. No account, no ads.

How it works

  1. Drop in your labels

    Drag PDFs or pictures onto the window, or browse for one or several. Each PDF page becomes one label, and blank pages are skipped.

  2. Check the preview

    White margins are trimmed off and every label is sized to exactly 4 × 6 in, turned to fit, with dashed lines to cut along.

  3. Print

    Pick your printer in the normal Windows dialog and keep the scale at 100%. Or save a print-ready PDF for later.

Reads PDF, PNG, JPG, GIF and WebP. Saved PDFs keep barcodes as sharp vector art.

Your labels stay on your computer

Pigeonslip has no network access, no accounts and no analytics. It only reads the files you give it and only writes the PDFs you save.
